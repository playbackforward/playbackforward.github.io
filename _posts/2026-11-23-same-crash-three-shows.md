Laptop A crashes halfway through a song. Same laptop, same crash. What happens next depends entirely on how the rig was built.

## One laptop

The tracks stop. The click stops. The band either stops too or plays on without the tracks, and the silence lasts until someone has restarted the laptop and found the right spot in the set.

## Two laptops, manual switch

Laptop B runs in sync, so the audio is there. But someone has to notice the crash, reach the switch and flip it. Until then, the PA plays nothing. That gap is short, maybe a couple of seconds<sup>*</sup>, and everybody in the room hears it.

## Two laptops, automatic failover

B was already playing. The failover device notices that A has stopped and switches over on its own. Nobody has to react, and nobody in the room hears a thing.

<small><sup>*</sup> Illustrative timing.</small>
